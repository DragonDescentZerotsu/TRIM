# Molecular-property playbook for TDC DILI classification

## neutral fraction
- Common threshold(s) or range(s): no stable literature threshold found
- Usually associated with: unclear (property alone is not a reliable separator of (A) vs (B))
- Brief note: For DILI-relevant **cholestatic** mechanisms, studies of bile salt export pump (BSEP) inhibition report that BSEP inhibition correlates with lipophilicity and that **positive molecular charge** is associated with *lack* of BSEP inhibition; however, DILI is multi-mechanistic, so “more neutral at pH” is not a stable rule for (A)/(B). citeturn9search4turn24view0turn20search19

## estimated logD
- Common threshold(s) or range(s): **proxy thresholds** commonly used in developability work: (i) “high lipophilicity” often treated as **logD7.4 ≳ 3** region (by analogy to logP-based DILI analyses and because logD7.4 is used in composite indices such as PFI); (ii) **PFI < 6** (PFI = Chrom logD7.4 + #Ar) is described as a desirable benchmark to improve solubility/developability (proxy). citeturn24view0turn14view0
- Usually associated with: (B) more likely when lipophilicity is high, but **only weakly and context-dependently** (often requiring other factors like dose/metabolism); otherwise unclear
- Brief note: The most task-relevant quantitative anchor for “lipophilicity-driven DILI risk” is actually expressed in **logP** (rule-of-two), but logD at physiological pH is often used as a more realistic surrogate for ionizable drugs; treat logD-based cutoffs as **proxies** rather than DILI-specific rules. citeturn25search0turn24view0turn14view0

## estimated logP
- Common threshold(s) or range(s): **DILI-focused anchor**: **logP > 3** is the “high lipophilicity” component of the rule-of-two (when paired with daily dose > 100 mg) used in multiple retrospective analyses; general drug-likeness proxy: **logP ≤ 5** (Rule-of-5). citeturn25search0turn24view0turn15search4
- Usually associated with: (B) more likely as logP increases (especially when other exposure drivers co-occur); (A) more likely at lower logP *in aggregate*
- Brief note: EASL notes that higher lipophilicity increases off-target binding and general toxicity risk and summarizes both supportive and non-supportive retrospective findings for the rule-of-two; treat **logP > 3** as a practical “risk-flag” rather than a deterministic separator. citeturn24view0turn25search0
- Source: citeturn25search0turn24view0turn15search4
